### 1. Gift & Packaging Boxes

**Q1: What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for custom wooden gift boxes?**
Our minimum order quantity (MOQ) for custom wooden gift boxes is 300 pieces per design. For first-time wholesale buyers testing a new product line, we can accept 100 pieces on stock-dimension boxes with standard finishes. Orders above 1,500 pieces qualify for volume pricing. The MOQ applies per unique design specification—if you need multiple sizes or finishes, each counts as a separate SKU.

**Q2: Which closure type is best for premium gift packaging?**
Magnetic closure is the modern standard for premium wooden gift boxes because it offers the cleanest exterior (no visible hardware) with a satisfying snap-close feel. For keepsake and jewelry-style boxes, hinged lids with concealed soft-close hinges provide a more traditional presentation. Sliding lids work best for high-volume programs where pack efficiency and cost are priorities. Your choice depends on the unboxing experience you want and your target retail price point.

**Q3: Can you match our brand's Pantone color on wooden gift boxes?**
Yes, we can color-match to any Pantone reference for painted or lacquered finishes. For natural wood finishes, we can match stain tones to color samples you provide, though exact Pantone matching isn't possible on porous wood surfaces. We recommend sending a physical color sample with your first order so we can create a finish standard for your account.

**Q4: What branding methods work best on wooden gift boxes?**
Laser engraving provides the cleanest, most durable mark and works on all wood species. Hot foil stamping (gold, silver, copper) is ideal for premium retail tiers and metallic branding. Debossing creates a tactile pressed effect. Screen printing handles multi-color logos and full-coverage artwork. UV printing allows photographic imagery and gradients. We recommend laser or hot foil for most gift box programs due to durability and premium appearance.

**Q5: Do your wooden gift boxes come with FSC certification?**
FSC chain-of-custody certification is available per order for brands that need it on their retail packaging labels. This is particularly important for German, French, and UK markets where retailers often require FSC documentation. The certification adds approximately 7-10 days to the sample approval timeline and requires advance notice when placing your order.

**Q6: What insert options are available for wooden gift boxes?**
We offer CNC-cut EVA foam (black or custom colors), velvet-wrapped trays in 12+ colors, microfiber linings, molded pulp inserts for eco-programs, ribbon pulls and dividers. Inserts are fully customizable to your product dimensions. For complex insert layouts, we recommend providing a sample of your product so we can engineer the perfect fit with zero movement during shipping.

**Q7: How long is the lead time for custom wooden gift boxes?**
Standard lead time is 30-40 days after sample approval for orders of 300-5,000 pieces. Sample production takes 7-10 days for stock patterns and 14-21 days for fully custom CAD designs. Rush production (20-25 days) is available for repeat orders with previously approved specifications. Add 7-10 days if FSC certification is required.

**Q8: What are the shipping terms for wooden gift boxes?**
We ship FOB Xiamen as standard, with EXW, CIF, and DDP available on request. ISPM 15 phytosanitary certification is included on every container so your shipment clears customs without delays. We consolidate 20-foot and 40-foot containers weekly for shipments to Germany, Italy, the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, and Poland.

### 2. Watch & Jewelry Boxes

**Q1: What lining options are available for watch boxes?**
We offer velvet lining (12+ colors), microfiber (scratch-resistant), faux leather, and suede. Velvet is most popular for luxury watch boxes. All linings can include embroidered logos. Watch pillow inserts are covered in matching material and sized to fit watches from 38mm to 50mm case diameter. For multi-watch boxes, we can mix lining colors per compartment.

**Q2: Can you make watch boxes with automatic winders?**
Yes, we manufacture watch boxes with integrated automatic winders for 1, 2, 4, 6, or 8 watches. Winder modules are Japanese or Swiss motors with adjustable rotation programs. These require electrical planning and add approximately 45-60 days to lead time. MOQ for watch winder boxes is 200 pieces due to the technical complexity.

**Q3: What wood species is best for jewelry boxes?**
Walnut is the premium choice for jewelry boxes due to its deep chocolate color and silky finish. Oak and acacia offer beautiful grain at a mid-tier price point. For larger jewelry chests, MDF with walnut veneer provides dimensional stability and keeps weight manageable. We recommend solid walnut for presentation pieces and veneered MDF for multi-drawer jewelry cabinets.

**Q4: Do your watch boxes have glass-top windows?**
Yes, glass-top or acrylic-window lids are available as an option. Mineral glass provides better clarity and scratch resistance. Acrylic is lighter and safer for shipping. Window openings can be full-lid or cut-out panels. This adds approximately $3-5 per unit to the base price depending on the window size and material choice.

**Q5: Can jewelry boxes include locks?**
Yes, we offer brass key locks, combination locks (3-digit), and cam locks. Locks can be surface-mounted (visible hardware) or concealed (clean exterior). For heirloom jewelry boxes and high-value watch storage, we recommend brass locks with brand-engraved keys. Lock options add approximately $2-4 per unit depending on type.

**Q6: What is the difference between soft-close and regular hinges?**
Soft-close hinges use a dampening mechanism that prevents the lid from slamming shut—it closes slowly and silently over the last 30 degrees of travel. This is essential for jewelry and watch boxes where the lid needs to feel premium and protect the contents. Regular hinges have no dampening. We recommend soft-close for all boxes over $30 retail price.

**Q7: How do you ensure the lid sits perfectly flat with zero gap?**
Every watch and jewelry box goes through a hand-fit quality check before packing. We use concealed European hinges with micro-adjustable mounting plates, allowing us to dial in perfect lid alignment. The lid and base are machined to tight tolerances (±0.2mm), and soft-close mechanisms ensure consistent closing pressure across the lid surface.

**Q8: What capacity configurations are available for watch boxes?**
We offer 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, and 24-watch configurations. Single-watch boxes are sized for one watch on a pillow. Multi-watch boxes can include drawer compartments for additional jewelry or accessories. Custom capacities (5-watch, 18-watch, etc.) are available on orders above MOQ 300 pieces with no tooling charges.
