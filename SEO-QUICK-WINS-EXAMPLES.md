## 一、类别页面SEO优化示例

### 1. Gift & Packaging Boxes

**当前：**
```javascript
seoTitle: 'Wooden Gift & Packaging Boxes — Wholesale & Custom OEM Manufacturer | CHIC',
seoDescription: 'Custom wooden gift & packaging boxes wholesale — magnetic, hinged & sliding-lid, debossed branding, foam/velvet inserts. MOQ 300, volumes to 100,000+. FOB Xiamen, ships to EU/US/UK/CA.',
```

**优化后：**

✅ 添加"China"（高搜索量B2B词）

✅ 突出"FSC certified"（买家关心的认证）  
✅ 保持在160字符以内

## 五、FAQ优化示例

### 为Tea & Coffee类别添加的FAQ：

```markdown
### What is the MOQ for custom bamboo tea boxes?
Our minimum order quantity (MOQ) for custom bamboo tea boxes is 300 pieces per design. For first-time wholesale buyers testing a new product, we can accept 100 pieces on stock-dimension sliding lid or hinged tea boxes. Higher volumes (1,500+ pieces) qualify for volume pricing.

### Which wood species is best for tea storage boxes?
Bamboo is the most popular choice for wooden tea boxes because it's naturally antibacterial, moisture-resistant, and carries a strong sustainability story (FSC certifiable, 5-7 year harvest cycle). For premium gift tea programs, walnut offers a luxury appearance. For high-volume subscription boxes, paulownia is the lightest option, reducing shipping costs.

### Can you make tea boxes with custom compartment layouts?
Yes, we offer standard 4, 6, 8, and 9-compartment configurations, and we can create fully custom layouts for orders over MOQ 300. Common custom requests include asymmetric compartments for different tea types, removable dividers, and mixed-depth wells for tea bags plus accessories.

### Are your wooden tea boxes food-safe?
Yes, all our tea and coffee boxes use food-safe water-based lacquer compliant with EU EC 1935/2004 and US FDA 21 CFR 175.300 standards. We can also provide raw or natural-oil finishes for brands seeking uncoated wood interiors. Compliance documentation is provided with every order.

### What's the lead time for custom wooden tea boxes?
Standard lead time is 30-40 days after sample approval for orders of 300-5,000 pieces. Sample production takes 7-10 days for stock patterns and 14-21 days for fully custom CAD designs. Rush production (20-25 days) is available for repeat orders with approved specifications.

### Do you offer FSC certification for bamboo tea boxes?
Yes, FSC chain-of-custody certification is available per order for brands needing it on retail packaging labels. This is particularly important for German, French, and Nordic markets. FSC documentation adds approximately 7-10 days to the sample approval timeline.

### Can you add acrylic windows to tea box lids?
Yes, we offer clear acrylic window inserts in tea box lids for retail visibility. This is especially popular for 8 and 9-compartment boxes displayed in cafes and retail environments where customers need to see fill levels without opening every box.

### What branding methods work best on bamboo tea boxes?
Laser engraving is the cleanest and most popular branding method for bamboo—it creates high contrast without any ink and suits eco-positioned brands. We also offer hot foil stamping (gold/silver) for premium retail tiers and screen printing for multi-color logos.
```

**这些FAQ的SEO价值：**
✅ 回答真实买家问题  
✅ 自然融入关键词（bamboo tea box, FSC certification, food-safe, etc）  
✅ 有机会获得Google "People Also Ask"展示  
✅ 增加页面内容深度  
✅ 降低跳出率（用户找到答案）

## 六、内部链接优化策略

### 原则：
每个产品/类别页面应该链接到3-5个相关页面

```javascript
related: ['bamboo', 'sliding-lid', 'kitchen-dining']
```

```markdown
... compartment tea boxes with acrylic windows. For eco-positioned brands, our

while [sliding lid wooden boxes](/products/sliding-lid) provide the most 
traditional hardware-free construction. Many cafe and kitchen retailers pair 
tea boxes with our [wooden kitchen storage](/products/kitchen-dining) range ...

... For premium gift tea programs, consider upgrading to a [walnut tea box](/products/walnut) 
with laser-engraved branding, or choose a [magnetic closure design](/products/magnetic) 
for subscription box programs where the unboxing experience drives repeat purchase ...
```

**优化理由：**
✅ 自然的contextual links（不是简单列表）
