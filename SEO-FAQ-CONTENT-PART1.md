## 1. Gift & Packaging Boxes - FAQ

### What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for custom wooden gift boxes?

Our MOQ for custom wooden gift boxes is 300 pieces per design. For first-time wholesale buyers testing a new product line, we can accept 100 pieces on stock-dimension boxes with standard finishes. Orders above 1,500 pieces qualify for volume pricing. The MOQ applies per unique design specification—if you need multiple sizes or finishes, each counts as a separate SKU.

### Which closure type is best for premium gift packaging?

Magnetic closure is the modern standard for premium wooden gift boxes because it offers the cleanest exterior (no visible hardware) with a satisfying snap-close feel. For keepsake and jewelry-style boxes, hinged lids with concealed soft-close hinges provide a more traditional presentation. Sliding lids work best for high-volume programs where pack efficiency and cost are priorities. Your choice depends on the unboxing experience you want and your target retail price point.

### Can you match our brand's Pantone color on wooden gift boxes?

Yes, we can color-match to any Pantone reference for painted or lacquered finishes. For natural wood finishes, we can match stain tones to color samples you provide, though exact Pantone matching isn't possible on porous wood surfaces. We recommend sending a physical color sample with your first order so we can create a finish standard for your account.

### What branding methods work best on wooden gift boxes?

Laser engraving provides the cleanest, most durable mark and works on all wood species. Hot foil stamping (gold, silver, copper) is ideal for premium retail tiers and metallic branding. Debossing creates a tactile pressed effect. Screen printing handles multi-color logos and full-coverage artwork. UV printing allows photographic imagery and gradients. We recommend laser or hot foil for most gift box programs due to durability and premium appearance.

### Do your wooden gift boxes come with FSC certification?

FSC chain-of-custody certification is available per order for brands that need it on their retail packaging labels. This is particularly important for German, French, and UK markets where retailers often require FSC documentation. The certification adds approximately 7-10 days to the sample approval timeline and requires advance notice when placing your order.

### What insert options are available for wooden gift boxes?

We offer CNC-cut EVA foam (black or custom colors), velvet-wrapped trays in 12+ colors, microfiber linings, molded pulp inserts for eco-programs, ribbon pulls and dividers. Inserts are fully customizable to your product dimensions. For complex insert layouts, we recommend providing a sample of your product so we can engineer the perfect fit with zero movement during shipping.

### How long is the lead time for custom wooden gift boxes?

Standard lead time is 30-40 days after sample approval for orders of 300-5,000 pieces. Sample production takes 7-10 days for stock patterns and 14-21 days for fully custom CAD designs. Rush production (20-25 days) is available for repeat orders with previously approved specifications. Add 7-10 days if FSC certification is required.

### What are the shipping terms for wooden gift boxes?

We ship FOB Xiamen as standard, with EXW, CIF, and DDP available on request. ISPM 15 phytosanitary certification is included on every container so your shipment clears customs without delays. We consolidate 20-foot and 40-foot containers weekly for shipments to Germany, Italy, the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, and Poland.

## 2. Watch & Jewelry Boxes - FAQ

### What lining options are available for watch boxes?

We offer velvet lining (12+ colors), microfiber (scratch-resistant), faux leather, and suede. Velvet is most popular for luxury watch boxes. All linings can include embroidered logos. Watch pillow inserts are covered in matching material and sized to fit watches from 38mm to 50mm case diameter. For multi-watch boxes, we can mix lining colors per compartment.

### Can you make watch boxes with automatic winders?

Yes, we manufacture watch boxes with integrated automatic winders for 1, 2, 4, 6, or 8 watches. Winder modules are Japanese or Swiss motors with adjustable rotation programs. These require electrical planning and add approximately 45-60 days to lead time. MOQ for watch winder boxes is 200 pieces due to the technical complexity.

### What wood species is best for jewelry boxes?

Walnut is the premium choice for jewelry boxes due to its deep chocolate color and silky finish. Oak and acacia offer beautiful grain at a mid-tier price point. For larger jewelry chests, MDF with walnut veneer provides dimensional stability and keeps weight manageable. We recommend solid walnut for presentation pieces and veneered MDF for multi-drawer jewelry cabinets.

### Do your watch boxes have glass-top windows?

Yes, glass-top or acrylic-window lids are available as an option. Mineral glass provides better clarity and scratch resistance. Acrylic is lighter and safer for shipping. Window openings can be full-lid or cut-out panels. This adds approximately $3-5 per unit to the base price depending on the window size and material choice.

### Can jewelry boxes include locks?

Yes, we offer brass key locks, combination locks (3-digit), and cam locks. Locks can be surface-mounted (visible hardware) or concealed (clean exterior). For heirloom jewelry boxes and high-value watch storage, we recommend brass locks with brand-engraved keys. Lock options add approximately $2-4 per unit depending on type.

### What is the difference between soft-close and regular hinges?

Soft-close hinges use a dampening mechanism that prevents the lid from slamming shut—it closes slowly and silently over the last 30 degrees of travel. This is essential for jewelry and watch boxes where the lid needs to feel premium and protect the contents. Regular hinges have no dampening. We recommend soft-close for all boxes over $30 retail price.

### How do you ensure the lid sits perfectly flat with zero gap?

Every watch and jewelry box goes through a hand-fit quality check before packing. We use concealed European hinges with micro-adjustable mounting plates, allowing us to dial in perfect lid alignment. The lid and base are machined to tight tolerances (±0.2mm), and soft-close mechanisms ensure consistent closing pressure across the lid surface.

### What capacity configurations are available for watch boxes?

We offer 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, and 24-watch configurations. Single-watch boxes are sized for one watch on a pillow. Multi-watch boxes can include drawer compartments for additional jewelry or accessories. Custom capacities (5-watch, 18-watch, etc.) are available on orders above MOQ 300 pieces with no tooling charges.

## 3. Tea & Coffee Boxes - FAQ

### What compartment configurations are available for tea boxes?

We offer 4, 6, 8, and 9-compartment standard layouts, plus custom asymmetric configurations on orders above MOQ 300. The most popular is the 8-compartment square grid for tea bag assortments. Removable dividers are available for boxes that need to switch between tea bag, loose leaf, and coffee pod storage. Compartment depth is 60-72mm as standard to accommodate most tea and coffee formats.

### Are wooden tea boxes food-safe?

Yes, all our tea and coffee boxes use food-safe water-based lacquer compliant with EU EC 1935/2004 and US FDA 21 CFR 175.300 standards. We also offer raw or natural-oil finishes for brands seeking uncoated wood interiors. Food-contact compliance documentation is provided with every order at no additional charge.

### Which wood species is best for tea storage boxes?

Bamboo is the most popular for tea boxes due to its natural antibacterial properties, sustainability story (FSC certifiable, 3-5 year harvest cycle), and moisture resistance. For premium gift tea programs, walnut offers a luxury appearance. For high-volume subscription boxes, paulownia is the lightest option and reduces shipping costs significantly.

### Can you add acrylic windows to tea box lids?

Yes, clear acrylic window inserts are available for tea box lids. This is especially popular for retail display where customers need to see fill levels without opening every box. Windows can be full-lid coverage or cut-out panels. Acrylic windows add approximately $1.50-2.50 per unit depending on size and whether it's a sliding lid or hinged lid construction.

### Do coffee pods fit in your wooden tea boxes?

Yes, our standard tea boxes have 60-72mm interior depth which accommodates Nespresso OriginalLine (37mm), K-Cup (62mm), Nespresso Vertuo (53mm), and Lavazza A Modo Mio (53mm) pods. On custom orders above MOQ 300, we can create asymmetric compartment depths—for example, four deep pod wells plus one shallow row for sugar packets and stirrers.

### What branding methods work best on bamboo tea boxes?

Laser engraving is the cleanest and most popular for bamboo—it creates high contrast without any ink and suits eco-positioned brands perfectly. Hot foil stamping (gold/silver) works for premium retail tiers. Screen printing handles multi-color logos. We don't recommend debossing on bamboo as the material's layered structure can splinter under deep pressure.

### Can tea boxes have different closure types?

Yes, we offer magnetic closure (modern, premium feel), hinged lid (traditional, daily use), and sliding lid (cleanest look, hardware-free). Magnetic closure is most popular for subscription boxes and gift sets. Hinged lids work best for daily kitchen use. Sliding lids are preferred for wedding favors and craft-style tea packaging.

### What's the lead time for custom bamboo tea boxes?

Standard lead time is 30-40 days after sample approval. Sample production takes 7-10 days for stock compartment patterns and 14-21 days for fully custom layouts. FSC certification (if required) adds approximately 7-10 days to the timeline. Rush production is available for repeat orders with approved specifications.

## 4. Wine & Whisky Boxes - FAQ

### What bottle sizes fit in your wine boxes?

Our standard wine boxes accommodate 750ml Bordeaux bottles (standard wine bottle shape). We also build boxes for magnum (1.5L), champagne, and specialty bottle shapes on custom orders. Foam cradles are CNC-cut to your exact bottle profile, so any bottle size or shape is possible above MOQ 300 pieces. Send us a bottle with your first order and we'll engineer a perfect-fit cradle.

### Do wine boxes include foam inserts?

Yes, precision-cut EVA foam cradles are included as standard on all wine and whisky boxes. Foam holds bottles with zero rattle during shipping. We CNC-cut cradles to your exact bottle profile in black foam (standard) or branded colors. For gift sets with glasses and accessories, we create multi-cavity foam layouts that hold everything securely.

### Which wood is best for rustic wine crates?

Pine is the traditional choice for rustic wine crates due to its affordable price point, light weight, and how well it takes wood-burning branding (the vintage burned-in logo). We use European Scots pine or New Zealand radiata. Knotty pine adds character for farmhouse-style packaging. Pine crates land at a unit cost that works for volume retail and wine clubs.

### Can you make wine boxes with rope handles?

Yes, jute rope handles are a popular option for rustic wine crates and gift boxes. We also offer leather handles, recessed wooden handles, and galvanized wire handles. Rope and leather handles add a traditional, gift-friendly look and make the box easy to carry. Handle options add approximately $0.50-1.50 per unit depending on material and style.

### What branding methods work for wine boxes?

Wood burning (pyrography) is the traditional method for rustic pine crates—creates a vintage burned-in mark that looks authentic. Laser engraving works on all wood species and provides crisp detail. Hot foil stamping handles metallic logos and vintage years. Screen printing covers multi-color artwork. We recommend wood-burning or laser for most wine box programs.

### Do you offer walnut wine boxes for premium spirits?

Yes, walnut is our premium option for limited-edition spirits, anniversary releases, and duty-free gifting. The deep chocolate grain justifies a top-tier retail price. Walnut wine boxes typically include brass hardware, foam-cut bottle cradles, and compartments for glasses and accessories. These sit at 3-4x the price of pine crates but command premium retail prices.

### Can wine boxes hold multiple bottles?

Yes, we manufacture 1, 2, 3, and 6-bottle configurations as standard, with custom layouts available above MOQ 300. Multi-bottle boxes include dividers or individual foam cradles so bottles don't touch during shipping. Six-bottle boxes are sized to standard case dimensions for easy stacking and shipping. Magnum bottles require custom-sized boxes due to their larger diameter.

### What certifications do you provide for wine boxes?

ISPM 15 phytosanitary certification is standard on every container—this is especially important for solid pine crates which customs scrutinizes closely. FSC chain-of-custody certification is available per order for eco-positioned brands. EU REACH testing on finishes and CARB P2 documentation for composite components are provided on request at no additional charge.

## 5. Kitchen & Dining Boxes - FAQ

### Are wooden kitchen boxes dishwasher-safe?

No, wooden kitchen boxes should be hand-washed only. Dishwasher heat and harsh detergents will damage the wood and finish over time. All our kitchen boxes use food-safe water-based lacquer or natural oil finishes that are safe for hand washing with mild soap and water. We recommend oiling bamboo and acacia boxes every 6-12 months to maintain their appearance.

### Which wood is best for bread bins?

Bamboo and acacia are best for bread bins due to their natural antibacterial properties and moisture resistance. Bamboo is lighter and more affordable. Acacia offers dramatic grain patterns. Both species handle daily kitchen humidity well. We don't recommend pine or paulownia for bread bins as softer woods can absorb moisture and odors over time.

### Can kitchen boxes have roll-top lids?

Yes, roll-top tambour lids are available for bread bins and larger kitchen storage boxes. These slide open horizontally and save counter space compared to hinged lids. Tambour slats are made from the same wood as the box body. Roll-top mechanisms require custom tooling and are only available on orders above MOQ 500 pieces. Lead time is 45-50 days.

### What sizes are available for wooden salt cellars?

Salt cellars typically range from 8×8×6cm (small countertop) to 12×12×8cm (larger capacity). We can manufacture custom sizes above MOQ 300 pieces. Most salt cellars use magnetic closure or a simple hinged lid. Bamboo and acacia are the preferred materials due to their moisture resistance—salt absorbs humidity and softer woods can warp.

### Do cutlery caddies come with handles?

Yes, cutlery caddies typically include a center handle for carrying from table to sink or storage. Handles can be solid wood (matching the box), jute rope, leather, or brass. Compartments are sized for forks, spoons, knives, and serving utensils. Dividers are removable on some models so the caddy can double as a general storage box.

### Are wooden kitchen boxes suitable for restaurants?

Yes, many of our kitchen and dining boxes are used in commercial settings—restaurants, cafes, bakeries, and hotels. We recommend bamboo or acacia for commercial use due to their hardness and moisture resistance. Food-safe lacquer finishes are essential for restaurant environments. For high-volume commercial orders (1,000+ pieces), we offer volume pricing and faster lead times.

### Can I get custom sizes for kitchen storage boxes?

Yes, custom dimensions are available on all kitchen boxes above MOQ 300 pieces. Common custom requests include specific drawer depths, countertop widths, and stacking heights. We can also create modular systems where multiple boxes nest or stack together. Send us your space dimensions and we'll engineer boxes that fit perfectly.

### What's included in food-safe certification?

Our food-safe finishes comply with EU EC 1935/2004 (European food contact regulation) and US FDA 21 CFR 175.300 (US food contact regulation). We provide test reports and declaration of compliance documents with every order at no additional charge. This documentation is required by many retailers and import/export authorities for kitchen and food storage products.

## 6. Sliding Lid Boxes - FAQ

### What is a dado groove and why does it matter?

A dado groove is a CNC-routed channel in the box walls that the sliding lid fits into. We route these grooves to ±0.3mm tolerance, which is what makes the lid slide smoothly without being loose or binding. This precision matters because the box will experience humidity changes during global shipping—our tight tolerances ensure the slide feels consistent from German winters to Florida summers.

### Can sliding lid boxes be made stackable?

Yes, sliding lid boxes are naturally stackable because of their flat lids. We can add registered edges or lip details that help boxes stack securely for retail shelving. This is especially popular for craft kits, tea assortments, and wedding favor displays. Stackable features don't require tooling charges on orders above MOQ 300 pieces.

### Which wood species is best for sliding lid boxes?

Walnut sits at the premium end (darkest stain, highest retail price). Pine is the affordable workhorse (clean engraving, $3-6 unit cost). Bamboo brings sustainability credentials (FSC certified, 5-7 year harvest cycle). Paulownia is lightest weight (half the shipping cost of pine). Your choice depends on your retail price point and whether freight weight matters to your program.

### Can you add finger pulls or brass tabs to sliding lids?

Yes, both are available as standard options. A routed finger pull on the front edge enables one-hand opening and adds no cost. Inlaid brass tabs provide a premium look and add approximately $0.80-1.20 per unit. For push-to-eject operation, we can add corner-mounted hidden magnets that convert the sliding lid into a spring-loaded mechanism ($2-3 per unit).

### Do sliding lid boxes work for food storage?

Yes, sliding lid boxes are excellent for tea, coffee, cigars, and dry food storage. We apply food-safe water-based lacquer (EU EC 1935/2004 + US FDA 21 CFR 175.300 compliant) for any box that will hold food contact. For cigar humidors, we line the interior with Spanish cedar. Airtight seals are not possible with sliding lid construction—use magnetic or hinged closure if airtight storage is required.

### Can you add acrylic windows to sliding lids?

Yes, we can cut a window opening in the sliding lid and install a clear acrylic panel. This is popular for tea boxes and craft kits where retail visibility matters. Acrylic windows add approximately $1.50-2.00 per unit depending on window size. The window can be full-lid coverage or a cut-out panel with wood framing.

### How do you brand sliding lid boxes?

The sliding lid surface is the largest brandable area in any wooden box structure. Laser engraving lands cleanly on walnut, pine, and paulownia. Hot foil stamping works for premium retail tiers (gold/silver/copper). Screen printing and UV printing handle full-color logos and photographic artwork. We recommend laser or hot foil for most sliding lid programs due to durability.

### What's the difference between sliding lid and lift-off lid?

Sliding lid boxes have a groove-and-rail system where the lid slides horizontally to open. Lift-off lid boxes have a separate lid that lifts straight up and can be fully removed. Sliding lids are more secure during shipping and retail handling. Lift-off lids provide a more ceremonial reveal for luxury gifts. Both are hardware-free constructions.
