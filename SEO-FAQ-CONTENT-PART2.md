## 7. Garden & Seed Boxes - FAQ

### What wood species is best for outdoor garden boxes?

Pine and cedar are the best choices for outdoor garden boxes due to their natural rot resistance and weather durability. Cedar contains natural oils that repel insects and resist moisture damage. Pine treated with outdoor-grade UV lacquer provides excellent weather protection at a more affordable price point. We don't recommend paulownia or bamboo for permanent outdoor use as they're better suited for indoor or covered patio applications.

### Do wooden garden boxes need drainage holes?

Yes, planter boxes need pre-drilled drainage holes to prevent water accumulation and root rot. Our standard planter designs include 3-5 drainage holes spaced evenly across the base. For seed storage boxes that won't hold soil or plants, we provide solid bases. Specify your intended use when ordering so we can configure drainage correctly.

### Can wooden seed boxes be customized with labeled compartments?

Yes, compartmented seed storage boxes can include custom labeling. We offer three labeling options: laser-engraved compartment dividers, brass label frames with paper inserts (replaceable for different seed varieties), or screen-printed labels directly on the box interior. The 9-compartment and 12-compartment layouts are most popular for organized seed packet storage.

### What finish is safe for vegetable planter boxes?

For planters growing edible plants, we use food-safe water-based lacquer or natural linseed oil finishes that comply with EU EC 1935/2004 and US FDA 21 CFR 175.300 standards. These finishes are safe for direct soil contact and won't leach chemicals into your vegetables. We provide compliance documentation with every food-contact order at no additional charge.

### How do you prevent wooden garden boxes from warping outdoors?

We prevent warping through three methods: selecting dimensionally stable wood species (cedar and treated pine), applying outdoor-grade UV-resistant finishes on all surfaces (including the base), and using proper joinery techniques that allow for natural wood movement. For large planters, we add corner brackets and reinforcing straps. Proper drainage also prevents moisture-related warping.

### Can garden boxes include handles for portability?

Yes, handles are available in jute rope, leather, galvanized wire, or recessed wooden grips. Rope and leather handles add a rustic garden aesthetic. Wire handles are most durable for heavy planters. Recessed handles keep the box stackable. Handle placement depends on box size—smaller herb planters get center handles, larger planters get side handles for two-person carrying.

### What sizes are available for wooden planters?

Our standard planter sizes range from small herb boxes (20×15×12 cm) to large raised bed planters (80×40×30 cm). Custom dimensions are available on orders above MOQ 300 pieces. Popular sizes for retail garden centers include: small (herbs and succulents), medium (vegetable seedlings), and large (patio planters for small trees or multiple plants).

### Do you offer FSC certification for garden boxes?

Yes, FSC chain-of-custody certification is available per order for garden centers and retailers selling under sustainability programs. This is increasingly important in German, UK, and Nordic markets where eco-conscious gardeners specifically seek FSC-certified wooden products. Certification adds approximately 7-10 days to the lead time and requires advance notice.

## 8. Storage Boxes - FAQ

### What makes wooden storage boxes stackable?

Stackable wooden boxes feature registered edges—either a lip that fits into the box below or precisely machined flat tops and bottoms that prevent sliding. We machine all mating surfaces to ±0.3mm tolerance so boxes stack securely without wobbling. Standard footprints (A4, A3, and custom shelf depths) allow mixing different box heights in the same stack.

### Can storage boxes include label frames?

Yes, brass label frames or recessed label panels are available on all storage box styles. Brass frames accept standard paper labels (75×30mm or custom sizes) that slide behind a clear window. This is popular for workshop tool storage, office supplies, and retail back-stock where contents change frequently. Laser-engraved permanent labels are also available.

### What's the difference between drawer cabinets and stackable boxes?

Drawer cabinets are fixed multi-tier units where drawers slide in and out on rails—ideal for jewelry, tools, or office supplies that need organized compartments. Stackable boxes are individual units that can be reconfigured and separated—better for storage that needs flexibility. Many customers use both: drawer cabinets for frequently accessed items, stackable boxes for seasonal or bulk storage.

### Do wooden storage boxes come with dividers?

Divider options include fixed dividers (permanently installed during manufacturing), removable dividers (slot into grooves so you can reconfigure), and modular grid systems (create custom compartment layouts). Foam inserts are available for tool storage where precise fit matters. Specify your storage needs when ordering so we can engineer the right divider system.

### Can storage boxes be made with casters for mobility?

Yes, lockable swivel casters are available for floor-rolling storage units. Casters are typically added to larger drawer cabinets and workshop storage boxes. We use industrial-grade casters rated for 50kg+ per unit. Locking mechanisms prevent rolling during use. Casters add approximately $4-6 per unit depending on load capacity and size.

### What wood is best for workshop tool storage?

Pine and birch plywood are the most cost-effective choices for workshop storage. Both are durable enough for daily use and easy to repair if damaged. For tool chests storing expensive hand tools, walnut or oak provides a premium look. Paulownia is ideal for large storage crates where weight matters—easier to move when full.

### How do you size storage boxes to fit standard shelves?

We manufacture storage boxes to common shelf depths: 25cm (standard bookshelf), 30cm (IKEA Kallax/Expedit), 35cm (Europallets), and 40cm (industrial shelving). Height options include 15cm, 20cm, 25cm, and 30cm to maximize vertical space. Custom sizing is available above MOQ 300 pieces—send us your shelf dimensions and we'll engineer boxes that fit perfectly.

### Can office storage boxes match corporate branding?

Yes, branding options include laser-engraved logos on drawer fronts or box lids, custom painted finishes to match corporate colors (Pantone matching available), and screen-printed logos for multi-color branding. Many office furniture programs use natural wood finishes with subtle engraved branding for a professional look. We can provide branded samples before production begins.

## 9. Hinged Boxes - FAQ

### What's the difference between surface-mount and concealed hinges?

Surface-mount hinges are visible on the exterior—typically brass or antique brass for rustic looks and traditional designs. Concealed hinges (European-style) hide completely inside the box, leaving a clean exterior with no visible hardware. Concealed hinges work best on jewelry boxes and premium gift packaging. Surface-mount hinges suit wine crates, tool chests, and vintage-style boxes.

### Do soft-close hinges work on all wooden box sizes?

Soft-close hinges work best on boxes with lids weighing 200g-2kg. Very light lids (under 200g) don't have enough weight to trigger the dampening mechanism. Very heavy lids (over 2kg) require upgraded soft-close systems with stronger dampeners. Standard soft-close hinges fit boxes from jewelry box size up to large presentation cases. We specify the right hinge system based on your lid dimensions and material.

### Can hinged boxes include both hinges and locks?

Yes, hinged construction works with all lock types: surface-mount brass locks with keys, concealed cam locks, combination locks, and magnetic catches. For premium boxes, we often combine concealed soft-close hinges with recessed cam locks—this gives a completely clean exterior with no visible hardware except the keyhole. Lock and hinge placement is engineered together so they don't interfere.

### What hinge finish options are available?

Standard finishes include solid brass (unlacquered, develops patina), lacquered brass (maintains shine), antique brass (aged look), chrome, matte black, and oil-rubbed bronze. Hinge finish should match other hardware on your box (locks, handles, corner brackets). Most customers choose brass for traditional looks and matte black for modern minimalist designs.

### Do piano hinges work better than butt hinges?

Piano hinges (continuous hinges running the full lid length) distribute stress evenly and prevent lid warping—ideal for large boxes, humidors, and heavy-use applications. Butt hinges (2-3 separate hinges spaced along the lid) are sufficient for smaller boxes and cost less. For boxes over 30cm wide, we recommend piano hinges to maintain lid flatness over time.

### Can hinged boxes open more than 90 degrees?

Yes, hinge opening angles range from 90° (lid stops at vertical) to 180° (lid folds completely flat). Most jewelry and gift boxes use 90-95° hinges so the lid stays upright for display. Tool chests and wine boxes often use 180° hinges so the lid can lay flat behind the box. Specify your preferred opening angle based on how the box will be used.

### How do you prevent hinges from rusting?

All our hinges are solid brass, stainless steel, or coated steel—none rust under normal conditions. Brass develops a natural patina (greenish oxidation) over decades but doesn't rust. Stainless steel remains corrosion-free indefinitely. For coastal or high-humidity environments, we recommend solid brass or marine-grade stainless hinges rather than coated steel.

### What's the lead time for custom hinge configurations?

Custom hinge configurations (non-standard opening angles, specialized soft-close mechanisms, or unique finishes) add approximately 7-14 days to standard lead time. This is because we engineer and test the hinge operation before production. Standard brass or concealed hinges in common sizes don't affect lead time—these are in-stock components we use daily.

## 10. Sliding Lid Boxes - FAQ

### Why choose sliding lid over hinged or magnetic closure?

Sliding lid boxes offer three advantages: lowest assembly cost per unit (no hardware), largest flat branding surface (the entire lid), and most compact shipping (boxes nest flat). They're ideal for high-volume programs where unit cost matters—craft kits, wedding favors, and subscription boxes. Trade-off: they don't have one-handed operation like magnetic boxes or the premium hinge feel of jewelry boxes.

### What is dado groove tolerance and why does it matter?

Dado groove tolerance is how precisely the lid fits into the grooved track. We CNC-route grooves to ±0.3mm tolerance—tight enough that the lid doesn't rattle loose, loose enough that it slides smoothly in all climates. Looser tolerances (±0.5mm+) create sloppy slides that rattle. Tighter tolerances (±0.1mm) can bind in humid climates when wood expands slightly.

### Can sliding lid boxes be made airtight?

No, sliding lid construction cannot create an airtight seal. The groove-and-rail design always has small gaps for the slide mechanism. For tea, coffee, and dry food storage, this provides adequate freshness protection but not true airtight storage. If you need airtight sealing for cigars or humidity-sensitive products, hinged boxes with silicone gaskets or magnetic boxes with compression seals are better choices.

### How do you add finger pulls to sliding lids?

Finger pulls are routed into the front edge of the lid during CNC machining—either a simple rounded groove or a decorative scalloped cutout. This adds no cost and enables one-hand opening. Inlaid brass tabs ($0.80-1.20 per unit) provide a premium look and easier grip. For push-to-eject operation, we install corner-mounted hidden magnets ($2-3 per unit) that convert the slide into a spring-loaded mechanism.

### Can sliding lid boxes have windows?

Yes, acrylic or glass windows can be cut into sliding lids. This is popular for tea boxes (see fill levels), craft kits (display contents in retail), and jewelry boxes (showcase pieces without opening). Windows add approximately $1.50-2.50 per unit depending on size and whether it's acrylic (lighter, safer for shipping) or mineral glass (scratch-resistant, higher clarity).

### What's the maximum size for sliding lid boxes?

We've built sliding lid boxes from tiny (60×40×15mm earring boxes) up to large (400×300×100mm sommelier presentation boxes). Practical maximum is around 50cm length—beyond that, the lid becomes difficult to slide smoothly and prone to binding. For very large boxes, we often use lift-off lids or hinged construction instead.

### Do sliding lid boxes work for cigar storage?

Yes, Spanish-cedar-lined sliding lid cigar boxes are a traditional design for 5-cigar to 25-cigar humidor-style storage. The cedar lining provides natural humidity regulation and aroma enhancement. However, sliding lids cannot create truly airtight storage, so they work best for short-term cigar storage (weeks to months) rather than long-term aging (years). For serious humidors, hinged construction with silicone gaskets is better.

### Can you brand the inside of sliding lids?

Yes, the underside of sliding lids can be laser-engraved, screen-printed, or lined with branded fabric. This creates a surprise branding moment when the box is opened—popular for subscription boxes and premium gift packaging. Velvet or microfiber lining in brand colors ($1-2 per unit) adds a luxury touch. We can also print messages, care instructions, or QR codes on the lid interior.

## 11. Drawer Boxes - FAQ

### What's the difference between wood-on-wood and ball-bearing slides?

Wood-on-wood slides are traditional—the drawer sides slide directly on waxed wooden rails. This is authentic, affordable, and easy to repair but requires periodic waxing. Ball-bearing slides use metal tracks with rolling bearings—they operate smoother, carry heavier loads, and never need waxing. Most customers choose wood-on-wood for jewelry and display (authentic feel) and ball-bearing for workshop tools and heavy storage (durability).

### Can drawer boxes include soft-close slides?

Yes, soft-close ball-bearing slides prevent slamming—the drawer gently decelerates over the last 2-3cm of travel and closes silently. This is essential for jewelry boxes (protects delicate items) and office furniture (professional feel). Soft-close adds approximately $2-3 per drawer. It requires ball-bearing slides—soft-close doesn't work with traditional wood-on-wood construction.

### How many drawer tiers can you build?

We manufacture 1-drawer jewelry boxes up to 12-tier apothecary cabinets. Most orders are 3-6 tiers for practical desktop height. Beyond 8 tiers, cabinets become too tall for comfortable use and require base stability features (wider footprint or bolt-to-wall brackets). Custom tier counts above 12 are possible but require structural engineering to prevent tipping.

### Can each drawer have different internal layouts?

Yes, each drawer in a multi-tier cabinet can have its own custom divider layout. Common configurations: top drawer with ring slots and earring posts, middle drawers with adjustable dividers for necklaces, bottom drawer open for larger items. For tool chests, we create foam-cut inserts matched to specific tool shapes. Custom layouts are included in the base price on orders above MOQ 300 pieces.

### What drawer pull styles are available?

Drawer pull options include brass cup pulls (traditional apothecary look), round knobs, recessed finger pulls (clean modern look), leather pulls, and brass label frames that double as pulls. Cup pulls and knobs add approximately $0.50-1.50 per drawer depending on style. Recessed pulls are CNC-routed during manufacturing and add no cost. Pull style should match your cabinet's overall aesthetic.

### Do drawer cabinets come with locks?

Yes, locks can be installed on the top drawer or on the cabinet door if the drawer unit is enclosed. Common setups: cam lock (single key locks all drawers via a locking bar), individual drawer locks (each drawer locks separately), or combination lock on the cabinet door. Most jewelry and watch storage boxes use cam locks—convenient single-key access for the owner.

### Can drawer cabinets be wall-mounted?

Yes, wall-mounting hardware is available for lighter drawer cabinets (1-4 drawers, under 5kg). We install keyhole mounting brackets or French cleats on the back panel. Heavy multi-tier cabinets (8+ drawers, over 10kg) should remain floor-standing with optional bolt-to-wall brackets for earthquake/tip-over safety. Specify wall-mounting requirements when ordering so we can engineer proper backing support.

### What wood species works best for drawer cabinets?

Walnut and oak are premium choices for furniture-grade drawer cabinets—beautiful grain, durable, smooth drawer operation. Pine is affordable for workshop and utility storage. Birch plywood provides excellent dimensional stability and is ideal for painted finishes (many office drawer systems use painted birch). Material choice depends on your aesthetic and budget—walnut for heirloom pieces, pine for working storage.

## 12. Magnetic Boxes - FAQ

### How strong should magnetic closure be?

Magnetic strength is calibrated to lid size and weight. Small jewelry boxes use N35 neodymium magnets (moderate hold, easy one-hand opening). Large gift sets use N42-N52 magnets (strong hold, secure in transit). We test every magnetic box design to ensure it opens one-handed without excessive force but stays closed when inverted or shaken during shipping.

### What's the difference between book-style and lift-off magnetic lids?

Book-style magnetic boxes have a hinged flap lid that folds back like a book cover and is held closed by magnets—the dominant style for subscription boxes and retail packaging. Lift-off lids separate completely from the base—more ceremonial reveal, popular for high-end jewelry and tech products. Book-style is more practical (lid can't be misplaced); lift-off is more dramatic.

### Can solid wood boxes have magnetic closures?

Yes, but MDF with wood veneer is more common for magnetic boxes because the magnets need to be precisely routed into thin walls (8-12mm). Solid wood requires thicker walls for strength, making magnet concealment more challenging. For premium magnetic boxes, we use solid hardwood walls with precisely machined magnet pockets. For cost-effective programs, veneered MDF provides a perfectly flat surface for full-color printing.

### Why use ribbon pulls with magnetic closures?

Ribbon pulls serve two purposes: they make opening easier (especially for strong magnetic closures), and they add a branded color accent. Grosgrain ribbon in brand colors ($0.30-0.50 per box) is most popular. Some luxury brands use leather or satin ribbon ($1-2 per box). Ribbon is functional but also part of the premium unboxing experience.

### Can magnetic boxes be made eco-friendly?

Partially—the magnets themselves aren't eco-friendly (neodymium mining has environmental impact), but the box structure can be. We offer FSC-certified wood and recycled-content MDF for the box body, water-based adhesives and lacquers, and molded pulp inserts instead of foam. This creates a "mostly eco" magnetic box, though it's not as sustainable as an all-wood sliding lid or hinged box with no magnets.

### What insert materials work best in magnetic boxes?

Molded pulp (recycled paper fiber) is most popular for eco-positioned brands—custom-shaped, affordable, and compostable. CNC-cut EVA foam provides precise product fit and premium cushioning. Velvet-wrapped trays create a luxury presentation. Choice depends on your brand position and product: eco brands use pulp, tech brands use foam, luxury brands use velvet.

### Do magnetic closures work for heavy products?

Yes, but magnet strength must be upgraded. Standard magnetic closures work for products under 500g. For heavier items (1-2kg tech products, glass bottles), we use multiple magnets or N52 grade (strongest available). Beyond 2kg, hinged or latched construction is more reliable than magnets—physics limits how much holding force magnets can provide in thin walls.

### How do you prevent magnets from falling out over time?

Magnets are permanently installed via three methods: precision-routed pockets with wood adhesive (magnets cannot physically escape), countersunk and wood-plugged (magnets held by wooden caps), or metal backing plates (magnets bond to concealed steel plates). We've never had a properly installed magnet fall out—failure only occurs with glue-only installation in low-cost production, which we don't use.

## 13. Lockable Boxes - FAQ

### What's the difference between cam locks and brass key locks?

Cam locks are recessed/concealed—just a small keyhole visible on the exterior, clean modern look. Brass key locks are surface-mounted—traditional hardware-visible aesthetic. Both offer equal security. Choice depends on design preference: cam locks for premium/modern boxes, brass locks for vintage/rustic boxes. Cam locks are slightly more expensive ($3-4 vs $2-3 for brass).

### Can combination locks be customized to specific codes?

Yes, 3-digit combination locks can be preset to your specified code before installation. Some customers use the same code across all boxes in a hotel or corporate program (easier for staff). Others request unique codes for each box in security applications. Resettable combination locks ($5-7 per unit) allow the owner to change the code later.

### Are wooden lockable boxes actually secure?

Wooden lockable boxes deter casual access—they're sufficient for home valuables, office petty cash, and medication storage where the goal is "keep honest people honest." They're not high-security safes. Determined thieves can break wooden boxes with tools. For serious security (large cash amounts, critical documents, controlled substances), metal safes are more appropriate. Lockable wooden boxes work for 95% of home and office use cases.

### Can you build lockable cash boxes?

Yes, cash boxes typically use reinforced construction: thicker walls (20-25mm vs standard 12-15mm), piano hinges or strap hinges (stronger than butt hinges), corner brackets, and either cam locks or brass locks. Interior dividers organize bills and coins. Sizes range from personal cash boxes (20×15×8cm) to business cash drawers (40×30×12cm). Commercial cash boxes often use cam locks for faster access.

### Do lockable boxes come with multiple keys?

Standard lockable boxes include 2 keys per lock. Additional keys can be ordered ($2-3 per extra key). For office and hotel applications ordering multiple boxes, we can key them alike (all boxes use the same key, $5 setup per order) or provide master key systems (manager keys open all boxes, individual keys open single boxes, $15-20 setup per order).

### Can you engrave custom text on keys?

Yes, brass keys can be laser-engraved with logos, initials, or short text (hotel room numbers, box IDs). This is popular for corporate and hotel programs where key management matters. Engraving adds approximately $1-2 per key. Keys can also be tagged with branded leather key fobs ($3-5 per key) for a premium look.

### What lock type is best for jewelry boxes?

Cam locks work best for jewelry boxes because they're concealed—the clean exterior doesn't distract from the box's design. For heirloom jewelry boxes, traditional brass locks with ornate keys add vintage character. Combination locks eliminate the risk of lost keys but add visual hardware. Most luxury jewelry brands choose concealed cam locks or no locks (relying on hidden magnetic catches instead).

### Are lockable boxes TSA-compliant for airline travel?

No, TSA requires either no locks or TSA-approved locks that their master keys can open. Our standard cam and brass locks don't meet TSA requirements. If you're designing travel cases, we can install TSA-approved combination locks ($8-12 per unit) that travelers can set their own codes on while TSA retains override access.

## 14. Paulownia Boxes - FAQ

### Why is paulownia wood half the weight of pine?

Paulownia is naturally low-density (~280 kg/m³) with large cell structures and high air content—it's literally a lightweight cellular wood. Pine is about 510 kg/m³, walnut 640 kg/m³. This density difference translates directly to shipping weight. A 500-piece order of paulownia boxes might weigh 120kg vs 240kg for pine—substantial savings on air freight or when shipping weight is tiered pricing.

### Does paulownia wood warp or crack easily?

No, paulownia is exceptionally dimensionally stable—it's one of the most stable woods we use. The straight even grain expands and contracts minimally across humidity changes. This matters for boxes shipped globally: paulownia boxes manufactured in China's coastal humidity arrive in Arizona's desert climate without warping. This stability is why paulownia dominates subscription box and gift packaging programs.

### Can paulownia be stained to look like walnut or darker woods?

Yes, paulownia's pale cream color accepts stains beautifully—you can achieve colors from light oak to dark walnut. The even grain means stain applies uniformly without blotchiness. Many subscription brands stain paulownia to dark brown or espresso for a premium look while keeping the freight weight advantage. Paint also adheres perfectly—painted paulownia boxes in brand colors are popular.

### Is paulownia strong enough for heavy items?

Paulownia is lightweight but still structurally strong. It handles everyday gift box contents (cosmetics, tea, small electronics) without issues. For very heavy items (wine bottles over 1kg, multi-piece tool sets), thicker walls or pine/oak construction is better. Paulownia's tensile strength is adequate—it just doesn't have the density to absorb impact loads that hardwoods do.

### Why choose paulownia over cardboard for gift packaging?

Paulownia provides the perceived value of wood (premium unboxing, keepable box that customers reuse) at only marginally higher cost than high-quality rigid cardboard. Freight weight is similar to rigid cardboard but paulownia feels more luxurious and photographs better in unboxing videos. For subscription boxes where the container drives repeat purchase, paulownia wins on perceived value.

### Can paulownia boxes be laser engraved?

Yes, paulownia laser-engraves exceptionally well—the even pale-cream grain provides high contrast for detailed logo and pattern work. Clean engraving is one of paulownia's key advantages over pine (which can scorch unevenly around knots) and bamboo (which sometimes splinters under laser). Engraving depth is precise from 0.3mm (subtle) to 2mm (deep sculptural effects).

### Does paulownia have a scent or odor?

No, paulownia is virtually odorless unlike cedar (distinctive cedar scent) or pine (resinous smell). This neutrality is important for tea boxes, cosmetics packaging, and food gifts where wood odor could interfere with product scents. The lack of natural oils and resins also means paulownia doesn't require as much finish sealing as oily woods.

### Is paulownia sustainable and FSC certified?

Yes, paulownia is highly sustainable—it grows to harvest size in 8-10 years (vs 30-60 for hardwoods) and regenerates from root systems without replanting. FSC chain-of-custody certification is available per order for retailers needing it on labels. Paulownia's fast growth and carbon sequestration make it one of the most environmentally friendly wood choices after bamboo.

## 15. Pine Boxes - FAQ

### What's the difference between Scots pine and radiata pine?

Scots pine (European) has tighter grain, fewer knots in clear grades, and slightly better rot resistance—preferred for premium pine products. Radiata pine (New Zealand) grows faster, is more affordable, and equally suitable for indoor use. Both species machine cleanly and accept finishes well. For export to EU markets, we typically use Scots pine; for cost-sensitive programs, radiata pine.

### Should I choose knot-free or knotty pine?

Knot-free (clear grade) pine provides an even surface for painting, staining, and clean engraving—this is the choice for retail gift boxes and premium wine packaging. Knotty pine adds rustic character—the knots and color variation create an authentic farmhouse aesthetic. Craft retailers and vintage-style brands often specifically request knotty pine for the character grain.

### Can pine boxes be used outdoors?

Yes, with outdoor-grade finishes. We apply UV-resistant exterior lacquer or natural oil with UV inhibitors to protect against sun, rain, and temperature swings. Properly finished pine boxes last years outdoors—seed storage boxes, garden tool caddies, patio storage. Unfinished pine shouldn't go outdoors—it will gray, split, and rot within months. Ask for outdoor-grade finishes when ordering.

### Why is pine more affordable than hardwoods?

Pine grows faster (harvest in 25-35 years vs 50-100 for walnut/oak), is widely available globally, and machines easily (less tool wear, faster production). These factors combine to make pine 40-60% less expensive than walnut or oak. For high-volume programs where unit cost drives decisions—wedding favors, craft kits, volume wine boxes—pine makes the margin work.

### Does pine take wood-burning better than other woods?

Yes, pine accepts traditional wood-burning (pyrography) beautifully—the soft even grain burns cleanly without excessive charring. Hardwoods like walnut and oak take more heat to burn and can char messily. This makes pine ideal for wine crates and rustic gift boxes where the wood-burned vintage mark is part of the brand aesthetic. Laser engraving also works well on pine.

### Can pine boxes be food-safe for pantry storage?

Yes, with food-safe finishes. We apply water-based lacquer compliant with EU EC 1935/2004 and US FDA 21 CFR 175.300 for boxes that will hold bread, dry goods, coffee, or tea. Unfinished pine can be used for dry goods packaging but untreated wood absorbs moisture and odors over time. Food-safe lacquer creates a sealed barrier while maintaining pine's natural appearance.

### What's the typical lead time for pine wine boxes?

Standard lead time is 30-40 days after sample approval for 300-5,000 piece orders. Pine wine crates are one of our highest-volume products, so we maintain steady production capacity. Rush production (20-25 days) is available for $0.50-1.00 premium per unit. Sample production takes 7-10 days for stock wine box dimensions, 14-21 days for fully custom bottle cradles.

### Can pine boxes match the strength of hardwood boxes?

Pine has lower density than hardwoods, but proper construction techniques compensate: thicker walls (15-18mm vs 12mm for hardwood), corner joinery (dovetails or reinforcing blocks), and quality hardware (piano hinges, corner brackets). A well-built pine box matches oak or walnut durability for normal gift box use. Pine only falls short in high-impact applications (heavy tool chests, shipping crates for fragile items).
